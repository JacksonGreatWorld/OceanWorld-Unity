1 游戏数据和追踪输入

本应用在您的 PICO 设备应用数据区保存关卡进度、星级、收藏、图鉴、成就和设置，用于继续游玩、展示收获和保留偏好。当前版本关闭在线排行榜、昵称输入和应用自建云同步。

为实现视角变化、划水、抓取、渔枪和菜单选择，本应用通过 PICO XR 接口处理头部位置与朝向、手部位置、关节姿态与手势等追踪输入。游戏业务功能不主动把上述玩法记录或追踪输入上传到天鹅运营的服务器，也不将其用于广告或用户画像。

应用运行时可能产生错误、性能和交互状态日志，由设备的日志与诊断机制处理。当前游戏业务功能不建立自动上传这些日志的服务。您选择向我们反馈问题时，可以自行决定是否提供截图或日志。

2 PICO 系统和 SDK

本应用使用 PICO XR SDK 提供显示、追踪与设备交互能力。PICO 设备系统、商店和 SDK 服务可能根据其功能、权限及您的设置处理设备、诊断等数据。这些处理应结合 PICO 的说明理解。

2.9.14 普通商店候选版通过 PICO Platform SDK 向 PICO 平台核实当前用户是否有权使用本应用。这项使用权校验需要连接 PICO 平台；PICO 可能按其 SDK 和平台规则处理校验所需的账号、设备及授权信息。校验通过后才可进入游戏，网络异常可能导致暂时无法验证使用权。

这项校验不新增昵称、头像或好友权限。本应用的游戏代码不读取登录令牌（Access Token），也不将校验结果或授权状态同步到自建服务器。本地进度保存与上述联网校验分别处理。

请查阅 PICO 隐私政策 和设备中显示的现行权限说明。本政策不替代 PICO 的告知，也不减少我们对本应用使用 SDK 所承担的责任。

3 广告和付费

通过 PICO 商店一次性购买本应用可能需要付费，具体价格以商店页面显示为准。当前版本游戏内不接入广告服务，不提供应用内购买。购买、支付和退款由 PICO 商店按其规则处理；本应用在游戏内不采集支付密码或银行卡信息。我们不向广告商出售或提供上述玩法与追踪数据。

4 保存 删除和您的选择

本地游戏记录保存在您的设备上，供后续游玩使用。您可以在游戏中查看进度、收藏与成就、调整可用设置，也可以通过设备系统提供的应用数据清除或卸载功能管理数据。

清除应用数据或卸载可能永久删除本机进度、收藏、成就和设置。 本应用不提供自建云存档恢复；系统备份及恢复效果取决于 PICO 平台和您的设置。

您可在系统中管理追踪等相关权限或功能；关闭后，依赖这些输入的游戏操作可能无法使用。有关查阅、复制、更正、删除、撤回授权或其他隐私权利请求，请通过上述邮箱联系我们。我们无法直接访问或远程删除您的本地存档。

如果您主动发邮件，我们会收到您的邮箱地址和您提供的内容，用于回复、处理请求或履行必要义务，并仅在完成这些目的所需的期限内保留。请勿发送密码、验证码、身份证件或与问题无关的敏感信息。 分享截图或日志前，请遮盖无关个人信息。

客服邮箱由 Gmail 提供，邮件经该服务处理与存储，相关服务安排和数据处理见 Google 隐私权政策。本政策网页由 GitHub Pages 托管，访问时 GitHub 可能处理 IP 地址等访问数据，见 GitHub 隐私声明。访问网页和游戏内本地存档属于不同场景。

请遵守设备及商店公布的年龄与使用要求。未成年人应在监护人指导下阅读本政策和使用产品；监护人如有隐私相关问题，可通过邮箱联系我们。

English

This policy covers the PICO Unity edition of OceanWorld, package com.picoxr.swan.underwater, version 2.9.13, and describes the additional entitlement check in the 2.9.14 store release candidate. We will update this policy if later versions change these data practices. Its public publisher/operator name is 天鹅.

Game data and tracking

The app stores progress, ratings, collections, encyclopedia entries, achievements and settings in its app-data area on your PICO device. The current version has no online leaderboards, nickname entry or app-operated cloud sync.

The app processes head position and orientation, hand position, joint poses and gesture inputs through PICO XR interfaces for the view, swimming, grabbing, speargun and menu interactions. Its game features do not proactively upload these gameplay records or tracking inputs to servers operated by 天鹅, or use them for advertising or profiling.

Runtime errors, performance and interaction-status logs may be handled by the device's logging and diagnostic facilities. The current game features do not provide an automatic upload service for these logs. You choose whether to share screenshots or logs when requesting support.

PICO services

PICO system, store and SDK services may process device or diagnostic data according to their functions, permissions and your settings. Review the PICO Privacy Policy applicable to your device and region. This policy does not replace PICO's notices or reduce our responsibilities for the app's use of the SDK.

The 2.9.14 store release candidate uses the PICO Platform SDK to ask PICO whether the current user is entitled to use this app. This check requires a connection to PICO. PICO may process the account, device and entitlement information needed for the check under its SDK and platform rules. Access to the game requires a successful check; network errors may temporarily prevent verification.

This check does not add nickname, avatar or friends permissions. The game's code does not read an Access Token or sync check results or entitlement status to an app-operated server. Local progress storage is handled separately from this online check.

Ads and payments

A one-time purchase of this app through the PICO Store may require payment; the price shown on the store page applies. This version has no in-game advertising services or in-app purchases. Purchases, payments and refunds are handled by the PICO Store under its rules. The app does not collect payment passwords or bank card information in the game. We do not sell or provide the gameplay or tracking data described above to advertisers.

Storage and your choices

Local records remain on your device for future play. You can view progress, collections and achievements in the app and manage available settings. Device app-data clearing or uninstall controls may permanently delete local records and preferences. The app offers no app-operated cloud-save recovery; system backup and restoration depend on the platform and your settings.

You can manage relevant tracking functions or permissions in system settings. Disabling them may prevent interactions that depend on them. Contact us about access, correction, deletion, withdrawal of consent or other privacy requests. We cannot directly access or remotely delete saves on your device.

Email and this website

If you email us, we receive your address and the information you provide for support or necessary obligations, retaining it only as long as needed for those purposes. Do not send passwords, verification codes, identity documents or unrelated sensitive information. Redact unrelated personal information before sharing screenshots or logs.

Gmail processes and stores support emails under Google's Privacy Policy. GitHub Pages hosts this policy website and may process visit information such as IP addresses under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Website visits are separate from local gameplay storage.

Minors updates and contact

Follow device and store age and usage requirements. Minors should review this policy and use the product with a parent or guardian. Guardians may contact us with privacy questions.

We update the date when practices change, communicate material changes through the store or app, and seek separate consent where required before the relevant processing begins.
